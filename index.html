PhoneRender privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026.

PhoneRender is an app for user-directed source capture, exports and rendering with a paired Mac. The app’s owner and developer is the privacy contact: comptecmaster@gmail.com.

Version scope. The separate Face Capture choices and single public render choice described below apply to the privacy successor currently in development. They do not establish that those controls are present in the currently installed PhoneRender 0.3.1 (15), or that the successor has been delivered. Existing Editor and pairing features are described separately. Contact the developer if you are unsure which version you are using.

Editor media and local projects

The Editor processes the photos, videos, audio and subtitle files you select, and camera media you choose to record. It copies imported originals into local projects and may create video previews, audio waveforms, foreground cutouts, exported movies, subtitles and on-device analysis results. Project records include edits, asset names and identities, checksums and saved revisions. These features use their own import, camera, editing and sharing actions; they do not use the separate TrueDepth Face Capture consent described below.

Editor processing runs on the iPhone. The Editor does not automatically upload your selected media to the developer. Sharing an exported file sends a copy to the app, person or storage destination you choose, which may include cloud storage. Removing a clip from a timeline does not guarantee deletion of its saved asset or older project revisions. The face-capture backup exclusion below does not describe all Editor project files; device and selected storage backup settings may apply to other data.

Pairing and connection information

When you choose to pair with a Mac, PhoneRender processes its invitation and recipient identity, local network address, certificate identity, grant expiry and access credential. Saved pairing credentials use this app’s device-only Keychain storage; the selected controller identity and address are also saved locally for explicit reconnect. Pairing information is used to authenticate the selected recipient and authorized connection.

You can remove a saved pairing locally. This does not revoke an already issued Mac-side grant: revoke that grant on the Mac separately. Removing a pairing is also separate from deleting captures, project files or copies already received elsewhere. Do not assume deleting the app revokes the Mac grant or removes all saved Keychain pairing material.

Your choices

Face capture is optional. The app asks for a separate collection choice and iOS camera permission before checking the camera or capturing. Capture completion keeps the source on the iPhone, including when the 60-second capture limit is reached. It does not automatically send or export the capture.

Use only your own face or the face of someone who has agreed to the capture and its intended sharing. Saved captures do not inherit a new collection, sharing or export choice.

Face information and its purpose

If you choose capture, PhoneRender retains original color images, depth planes, camera calibration, timestamps, capture settings and camera or pairing identifiers. These may contain identifiable face information. The purpose is preserving source for your directed reconstruction and exports.

PhoneRender does not use face data for Face ID authentication, advertising, profiling or AI training. Derived output can be incomplete; it is not a promise of likeness or measurement accuracy.

Where information goes

Originals and app-prepared exports remain in the app’s local storage. Face information is not automatically sent to the developer. Sending to a Mac requires a separate choice for the exact retained capture revision and the saved HTTPS recipient identity. Pairing alone does not authorize that transfer.

Files export is a separate choice. The document picker copies the prepared package to the destination you select. That destination may synchronize to a cloud service. A derived package includes all original color images, depth, calibration, timing and provenance files alongside partial derived output. If derivation is unavailable, the prepared package may instead contain the original-source fallback. The final Files choice identifies the actual prepared kind, capture and revision.

Recipients and storage services control copies after they receive them. Manage those copies, their access and any backups separately.

Retention, withdrawal and removal

Captured originals and app-prepared exports remain through stopping, relaunch and successful delivery. Capture storage is marked as excluded from device backup. There is no automatic retention deadline or per-capture deletion control in this version. Storage limits stop new capture rather than removing earlier originals.

Withdrawing consent prevents new capture, transfer, export and render work and waits for active operations to drain. Choices reset when the relevant screen or session ends or the app becomes inactive. Already committed originals and valid delivery receipts remain stored. Withdrawal does not recall completed transfers or Files copies.

iOS Delete App removes the app and its local app data; Offload App retains documents and data. Deleting the app also affects its projects and settings. This is not selective capture deletion or a secure-erasure guarantee. Deleting app data does not remove copies on a Mac, in Files or in a recipient’s backups.

For privacy questions or help identifying access and removal options, contact comptecmaster@gmail.com. Emailing the developer does not grant remote access to your phone or erase its files. Please do not attach face images or capture packages to an initial request. Your email service and the developer’s email service handle the messages and attachments you choose to send.

The first public render test

Render consent is separate from face capture and pairing. The first test authorizes one named public synthetic Moon job from the verified Mac, with a fixed input identity, a first attempt and a temporary grant. It does not need face captures or private production assets.

The job uses the phone’s GPU, CPU, battery, memory and network, and may warm the phone. It returns a color PNG and a receipt containing job, input and output identities and measured durations to the paired Mac. Keep the app open. Declining or revoking the choice stops new work and drains active operations. This choice does not authorize additional or third-party-benefiting jobs or a general queue.

Changes and contact

Review the policy and the app’s current disclosure before making a choice. Material changes to what an action permits need a new explicit choice; earlier consent is not blanket authorization for future work.
